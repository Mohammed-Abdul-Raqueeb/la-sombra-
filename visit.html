Getting here

Fifty minutes from the airport, then a private track.

  1. Fly in

    The nearest airport is fifty minutes by road. Most people arrive mid-afternoon, which works well — you will be at the west terrace by the time it turns.

  2. Let us send a car

    Tell us the flight and we will meet it. €70 each way, the same as a taxi, and the driver knows the track.

  3. Or drive

    Two hours from the city on good road. The last two kilometres are our track — passable in anything, slowly. There is parking in the car court.

  4. Arriving late

    There is no night reception. If you land after ten, say so and we will leave the room open, a key on the sill and something cold in the fridge.
