Hours

The estate keeps solar time.

Everything here moves with sunrise rather than the clock, so these times shift by about ninety minutes across the year. What does not change is the order.

  1. 05:40

    First light

    The headland path is opened. Coffee and a thermos are left on the courtyard sill for anyone walking out early.

    in the sun
  2. 07:00

    Breakfast begins

    Under the east arcade, which takes the low sun until nine and is out of it after. Bread from the wood oven, fruit from the terraces, eggs if you want them.

    in the sun
  3. 09:15

    The gardeners move

    Watering finishes and the crew shifts to the north beds. The pool deck is quiet and already warm.

    in the sun
  4. 11:00

    Sea hour

    The boat leaves the cove for the sea caves and returns before the wind turns. Six places, first asked.

    in the sun
  5. 13:00

    The long table

    One sitting, one menu, four courses. It runs about two hours and nobody hurries it.

    in the sun
  6. 14:30

    Siesta

    The whole estate goes quiet. Shutters are drawn on the west side. Nothing is served, nothing is scheduled.

    in the shade
  7. 16:30

    Shade returns west

    The palms throw across the west terrace and it becomes the best seat on the property. Iced tea, cold almonds.

    in the shade
  8. 18:20

    Golden hour

    The light comes in flat under the canopy. The bar opens on the lower terrace.

    in the sun
  9. 19:45

    Sundown

    From the headland bench, which seats four and is worth walking to.

    in the sun
  10. 20:30

    Dinner

    Small plates on the courtyard, or the full menu at the table. Both run until it is finished.

    in the shade
  11. 22:30

    Lamps down

    Path lights drop to their lowest setting so the sky comes back. The bar stays open, quietly.

    in the shade

Booked separately

Four things worth putting in the diary.

  • The cove boat — 11:00, six places, ninety minutes. Ask at breakfast.
  • Kitchen morning — Tuesdays and Fridays, 09:00, four people, with Rosa.
  • The headland walk — 07:30 on Sundays, two hours, guided by whoever is free.
  • Cellar tasting — 18:30, six regional growers, on request and often at short notice.
